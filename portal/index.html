About the lab

A practical path into GenAI

This lab brings together focused, runnable demonstrations of generative AI concepts. Each practical keeps its own implementation, dependencies, and framework; the portal connects to those applications without merging their Python runtimes or replacing their functionality.

Some experiments need model files, credentials, compatible hardware, or an approved runtime. Their cards show setup guidance and honest status so you can choose the right next step.
